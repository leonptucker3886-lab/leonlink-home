How to read a spread without scaring yourself

Ask one question with an actual answer in it. "Should I leave?" is a decision you make; "What am I not seeing about this job?" is a question a spread can answer.

Read the three cards as one sentence, not three separate verdicts. If the Past is heavy and the Future is light, the sentence is about release, not doom. If two cards share an element or a number, that is the loudest thing on the table.

Write down what you understood before you redraw. Redrawing until you like the answer is not divination, it is shopping.

Where these meanings come from

The Major Arcana attributions follow the Golden Dawn tradition: planets and signs assigned card by card, which is why The Emperor is Aries and The Empress is Venus. Numbers follow the Pythagorean system, with the master numbers 11, 22 and 33 kept rather than reduced.

Planet positions come from simplified orbital elements and can be off by a fraction of a degree — enough to matter only if you were born on the cusp of a sign. The Moon uses a truncated series and is generally within about half a degree.
