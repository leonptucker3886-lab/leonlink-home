SITE 1The Big Idea

Kids Corner is one friendly, mobile-first town that kids explore with their thumb: one hub map, five zones around a Central Plaza, and a Grown-Ups Gate for parents. Every page is 3 taps or fewer from the hub.

Everything is original, from the Paper Trooper and General Pip universe. No casino, no ads aimed at kids, and no data collected from kids.

PERMIT: MOBILE-FIRST · ORIGINAL ART ONLY · KID-SAFE ZONING

SITE 3Architecture: The City Map

The hub lives at /kids. Each zone has its own landing page and sub-categories. Shared utilities run under the whole city: one sound toggle, one loading screen (the Normal intro, or the Arcade intro for arcade games) and one save system stored on the device.

ZONING: HUB → ZONE → ITEM · MAX 3 TAPS
SITE 4Street Layout

On phones: big tap tiles in a 2-column grid, the plaza dead centre, and a bottom nav bar for one-thumb use.

On desktop, the same map scales up into a wide illustrated town.
