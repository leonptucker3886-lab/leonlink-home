Ledgerline, the one to start with

Ledgerline runs a small trades business from a phone: quote it before you leave the driveway, let the customer approve and pay in their own portal, then take the card on the spot. It schedules the work, sends the invoices, and chases what is owed automatically, so nothing sits unpaid because you were busy.

Pricing is one flat rate per business, not per person. Adding a second man or a third does not raise the bill. Inspection checklists with photo proof, and a one-tap review request after every job, sit on top of it.
