Help or harm?

4. My take

Help or harm was always the wrong question. Fire helps and harms; the question is who holds the extinguisher. With AI, the extinguisher is alignment research, and it is currently losing the race against capability. That is not a reason to panic. It is a reason to fund the firefighters.
