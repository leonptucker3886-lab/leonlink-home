The 10 checks

Tick what passes

Slow pages lose half their visitors before the first pixel. Compress images, skip the video background.

If the headline is 'Welcome to our website', you failed. Mine says: Everything I build, in one place.

Two equal buttons is zero decisions. Pick the one that pays and make it the biggest thing on the page.

On mobile, more than half your visitors are one thumb-tap from calling you. Do not make them memorize digits.

Google ranks the phone version first. If your site is desktop-only, you are invisible to most of the internet.

Browsers now shame non-secure sites in front of your customers. Free certificates exist. There is no excuse left.

Stock photos of handshakes smell like a scam. One real photo beats ten perfect fakes.

Nothing says 'abandoned' like placeholder text. Dead pages are worse than no pages.

For local work, the Google listing matters more than the website. Claim it, fill it, feed it photos.

Every extra step halves the people who finish. Put the contact where thumbs already are.
