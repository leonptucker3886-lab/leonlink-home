Send in the clowns

5,000 YEARS OF FACE PAINT

Before the punchline, there was the painted face. Clowns have been with us for about five thousand years — court jesters, traveling players, circus kings, TV mascots.

And for the last fifty? Nightmares.

So here's the deal: the timeline, the famous faces, the dark side, and the actual science of why your skin crawls — then you vote.

5. My take

Five thousand years and the clown keeps surviving, because the paint is a license. The jester could insult the king. Grimaldi could fall down for a living. The mask lets a person say what nobody else is allowed to say — and do what nobody else is allowed to do. That's the whole trick.

The dark side is just the other reading of the same fact: you can't tell where the act ends. Same mask, same grin, same hidden face. Funny and scary were never opposites for the clown. They were always the same job.
