Why everyone's talking about it

Casio released the G-Shock 2500 Series (GAC-2500 and GMC-2500), and watch fans nicknamed it the CasioNaut within days. It's an all-analog G-Shock with an octagonal bezel that reminds people of the Patek Philippe Nautilus, one of the most hyped luxury sports watches on earth.

It's the same trick that made the GA-2100 "CasiOak" a cult hit: luxury design on a tough, affordable watch. Gear Patrol, G-Central and Notebookcheck covered the release this week, and Casio lists 9 models for the US and UK.
