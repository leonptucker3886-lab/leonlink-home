# Bert Kart - design notes (v1, 26 Oct 2026)
Audience 10-12. Guide: Bert. No ads, logins, chat or real-money purchases. Saves in localStorage only.
## Loops
- Second to second: steer, hold GO, hit yellow boost pads, grab coins, stay off grass (speed x0.5).
- Session: 3-lap race (about 1.5-2.5 min) -> medal + coins -> Garage upgrade -> race again vs Ghost Bert.
- Long term: unlock Dune Dash (any medal on Meadow), Neon Night (any medal on Dune), max 3 upgrades x3 levels, gold on every track.
## Ghost Bert
Until you finish a race, Ghost Bert drives the racing line at gold time +15%. After that, your best race becomes the ghost.
## Economy [PLACEHOLDER numbers, tune after playtest]
- Earn: 1 coin per track coin (30 per race), medal bonus bronze 10 / silver 20 / gold 30, +15 for a first or better medal.
- Daily cap 150 racing coins. Full upgrade set costs 1,000 coins (about 7-10 days of play).
- Medal times: Meadow 72/82/95 s, Dune 108/122/140 s, Neon 120/136/155 s.
## Safety
Pause on tab hide, mute button, reduced-motion respected, Kids Corner button always visible.
