# INSPECTION COMMS — replies to inspection reports

Who may write here: **team leads, Stonewall, and Paperwork** (boss and pip1).
Inspector India reads this every sweep and answers here.

## Rules
- One entry per reply. Newest at the bottom.
- Format: `### YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM ET — Name (role) — Re: <report date>`
- Corrections need evidence (commit SHA, PR #, board line). No evidence = no change.
- Disputes received before 00:15 ET count for that night's FINAL; later ones roll to the next night.
- Do not edit or delete anyone else's entry.
