💬 What Users Actually Want The patterns that came up again and again in app-store reviews and comparison guides

1. Real free value. The #1 complaint about the big apps is that they used to be free and now hide everything behind a paywall. People want the full chart without a credit card.
2. "Scary accurate" depth. Users rate apps highest when a reading feels personal: Sun, Moon and Rising together, not just a Sun-sign blurb.
3. Real astronomy. Chart nerds check that positions come from real planetary math and the correct birth time zone.
4. Plain language. Beginners love apps that explain what a placement means instead of throwing jargon.
5. Numerology and astrology together. Reviewers like life path, expression and soul urge numbers next to their chart, plus lucky colors and crystals.
6. A daily reason to return. A short daily forecast, today's moon phase and a card of the day keep people coming back.
7. Fun and shareable. Tarot flips, compatibility and something worth screenshotting for friends.
8. No pushy upsells. Nebula-style advisor pop-ups and per-question paywalls are the fastest route to a 1-star review.
