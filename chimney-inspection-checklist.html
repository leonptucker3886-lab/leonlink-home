Level 1 — visual, accessible portions

  • Firebox: damper opens/closes, no cracked firebrick, no gaps
  • Smoke chamber: parged, no visible gaps or corrosion
  • Lintel and hearth: sound, proper clearances
  • Fireplace surround and mantel: clearances to combustibles
  • Chimney exterior: no cracked/spalling brick or mortar joints
  • Crown: no cracks, proper overhang and drip edge
  • Flue tile (where visible): no cracked or missing sections
  • Chimney cap: present, secured, screened
  • Flashing: sealed at roofline, no rust or lift
  • Clearance to nearby trees and roof lines
  • Creosote stage noted: ______ (1st / 2nd / 3rd degree)
  • Draft test performed: smoke rises and clears

Level 2 — camera / hidden areas (real estate transfers, changes of use, after events)

  • Full flue scan: linear continuity, no breached tiles or liner joints
  • Smoke chamber behind damper shelf (camera view)
  • Attic/interior chase: no framing contact, no heat damage signs
  • Connector pipes: slope, joints, clearances correct
  • Prior glue/patch/tar work noted and photographed
  • Photos attached to this report (recommended for every finding)

Findings & recommendations

  • Pass — no service needed this visit
  • Cleaning recommended: ______
  • Repair needed: ______________________________________
  • Safety concern — do not use until: __________________
This checklist reflects common NFPA 211 practice for residential masonry and factory-built fireplaces. It is a field aid, not a substitute for the standard. Inspector judgment applies.
